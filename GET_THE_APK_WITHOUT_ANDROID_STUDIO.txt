GET THE APK WITHOUT INSTALLING ANYTHING (free GitHub account needed)

1. Go to github.com, sign in, press "+" > New repository. Name it endocrine-qbank. Keep it Private. Create it.
2. On the new repository page press "uploading an existing file".
3. Unzip Android_APK_Project.zip on your computer. Open the unzipped folder and drag
   EVERYTHING inside it (app, gradle, .github, build.gradle, settings.gradle, gradle.properties ...)
   into the GitHub page. IMPORTANT: upload the contents, not the folder itself.
   (The hidden ".github" folder must be included. On Windows: View > Show hidden items.
    On Mac: press Cmd+Shift+. in Finder.)
4. Press "Commit changes".
5. Open the "Actions" tab. A job called "Build APK" starts by itself (about 5 minutes).
   If it does not start: Actions > Build APK > Run workflow.
6. When it turns green, open it and download "Endocrine-Qbank-APK" at the bottom.
   Unzip it: app-debug.apk is your app.
7. Send app-debug.apk to the phone (WhatsApp, email, USB). Open it and allow
   "Install unknown apps" for that app when Android asks.

The app then works completely offline. Progress stays on the phone.
If step 5 shows a red cross, open the failed step, copy the last 20 lines of the error and send them to me.
